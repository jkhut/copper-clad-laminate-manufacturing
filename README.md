# Enhancing Quality & Efficiency in Copper Clad Laminate Manufacturing

> A data-driven approach to defect reduction and process optimization in PCB substrate manufacturing.  

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Copper-clad laminates (CCLs) are the foundational materials used in printed circuit boards (PCBs). This project tackled a real-world manufacturing challenge: a customer rejection rate of **1.29%**, more than double the industry target of **0.5%**.

Using a combination of statistical quality control tools and data-driven analysis, the team systematically identified root causes of defects and proposed actionable recommendations to optimize the CCL manufacturing process.

---

## 🏭 Industry Partner

This project was conducted in collaboration with **Isola Group**, a global leader in the manufacturing of copper-clad laminates for the electronics industry.

---

## 🔍 Problem Statement

The CCL manufacturing process was experiencing elevated customer rejection rates driven by four primary defect categories:

| Defect Type     | Contribution |
|-----------------|-------------|
| Hi-Pot Failures | 38.56%      |
| Surface Defects | 35.37%      |
| Voids           | 19.56%      |
| Foreign Material| 6.51%       |

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Evaluate and map the full CCL manufacturing workflow
- Identify stages most prone to defects
- Apply statistical QC tools to uncover root causes
- Propose improvements to reduce scrap rate to ≤ 0.5%

---

## ⚙️ Manufacturing Process Stages

```
Raw Materials → Impregnation → Ply-Up → Lay-Up → Pressing → Break-Down → Finishing
```

Each stage was analyzed for quality control metrics including resin distribution, curing consistency, thickness uniformity, and surface integrity.

---

## 🛠️ Methods & Tools Used

### Statistical Analysis
- **Pareto Charts** — Prioritized defect types by frequency and impact
- **Fishbone (Ishikawa) Diagrams** — Root cause categorization across Material, Method, Machine, and Environment
- **Control Charts** — Monitored process stability and identified out-of-control conditions
- **Regression Analysis** — Quantified relationships between curing time, pressure, and resin flow

### Quality Control Tools (7 QC Tools)
- Histograms, Scatter Diagrams, Check Sheets, Flowcharts

### Inspection & Testing
- Automated Optical Inspection (AOI)
- Thermocouples for real-time curing monitoring
- Destructive Physical Analysis (DPA) and Destructive Material Testing (DMT)
- High Potential (Hi-Pot) Testing

---

## 📊 Key Findings

- **Total value-added time:** only 38.2 seconds across all production stages
- **Major bottlenecks:** 15-day delay at the receiving stage, 12-day delay post-treating
- **Downward defect trend** observed from Week 23 onward, indicating process improvements were taking effect
- Hi-Pot failures and surface defects identified as the highest-priority areas for improvement

---

## ✅ Recommendations

1. **Optimize resin distribution** to reduce voids and delamination
2. **Improve curing consistency** through better thermocouple monitoring and press calibration
3. **Strengthen contamination controls** during impregnation and lay-up stages
4. **Streamline material transitions** using JIT inventory and automated scheduling
5. **Enhance Hi-Pot defect analysis** with deeper material characterization
